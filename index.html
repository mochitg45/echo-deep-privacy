Privacy Policy — Echo Deep

Effective 1 October 2026. Applies to the Android app com.inatasunsoft.echodeep, published by Inata Sun Soft.

Echo Deep is a single-player game. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and the game does not use your camera, your photos, your contacts or your location. This policy explains what stays on your device and the small amount of data that the services inside the app handle.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your progress (deepest dive, high score, pod, krill, songs and costumes you own, your Daily Dive result and your settings) is saved in your device's private app storage and stays there. We run no server and keep no database of players. Uninstalling the app deletes your progress, unless you use Play Games cloud save (see below).

Camera, photos and other permissions

None. The app asks for no camera, microphone, photo, storage, contacts or location permission. It uses the internet only for the services in the table below, and the vibration motor for short haptic pulses (you can switch this off in Settings).

Advertising choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before the first ad request, and you can reopen it from "Ad privacy options" in the game's Settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service above, contact us and we will help you raise it with that service.

Your rights and how to request data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. Everything the app stores is on your device and is deleted by uninstalling it. For data held by AdMob, reset your Advertising ID or use Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. To manage or delete your Play Games profile, scores and cloud saves, go to play.google.com/games/profile. For purchase records held by Google Play, see your Google Account.
